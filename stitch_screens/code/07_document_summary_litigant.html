ORDER BELOW EXHIBIT 5:
1. Heard the learned counsel for the Plaintiff and the learned counsel for the Defendants.
2. Perused the records, prima facie case is made out regarding registered ownership entry in respect of Gat No. 42/3 of Village Baner.
3. The ad-interim injunction granted earlier vide order dated 14-01-2024 is hereby extended until further orders.
4. The Defendants are hereby restrained by themselves, their agents, servants or any person claiming through them from carrying out any construction, alienation, creating third party rights or altering the physical boundaries of the suit property.
5. Defendant No. 1 is strictly directed to bring and file the original Title Deed dated 12-08-1998 on or before the next date without fail.
6. Both parties to maintain status-quo as on today.
7. Matter is posted for filing of Defendant's written say and compliance of direction No. 5 on 18-04-2025.

Dictated and pronounced in open court.
Sd/-
(S. K. Kulkarni)
Civil Judge Senior Division, Hall 4, Pune
