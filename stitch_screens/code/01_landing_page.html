Smt. Meenakshi S. Deshpande

Next Hearing / पुढील तारीख

18 April 2025 (Written Submissions)

description Plain English Summary Reading Level: Grade 7

1. Court Restraining Order Maintained: The civil judge has continued the interim injunction order. The developer cannot sell, construct, or transfer any rights over Survey No. 42/3 till the dispute is decided.

2. Production of Original Title Deed: The builder’s advocate must bring the original 1998 registered agreement to court on or before the next date.

3. Litigant Appearance Not Mandatory: Your presence is dispensed with for the next hearing since only document scrutiny will take place.
