Smt. Meenakshi Deshpande ... Plaintiff
Versus
M/s Sahyadri Land Promoters LLP & 3 Others ... Defendants

ORDER BELOW EXHIBIT 5
(Application for Interim Injunction under Order XXXIX Rule 1 & 2 r/w Section 151 of CPC)

[Para 1] The Plaintiff has instituted the present substantive suit seeking decree of perpetual injunction and declaration of rights concerning land bearing Survey Number 49/2A, admeasuring 2 Hectares 14 Ares situated at Mauje Baner, Taluka Haveli, District Pune.

[Para 2] Heard learned Advocate Shri S. Sharma appearing for the Plaintiff and learned Advocate Shri N. V. Deshmukh for Defendant Nos. 1 and 2. Perused the verification affidavit, copies of 7/12 extract extracts from 1985 to 2023, mutation entry No. 11048, and the registered development agreement dated 14.08.1998.

[Para 3] Upon careful scrutiny of prima facie evidence, this Court is of the considered opinion that the Plaintiff has successfully established a tripartite test: a strong prima facie case, balance of convenience lying in their favour, and imminent risk of irreparable loss should unauthorized multi-story construction proceed pending final adjudication.

[Handwritten Marginal Note Certified by Registrar] [Para 4] It is hereby ordered that Defendant Nos. 1 and 2, their partners, servants, agents, contractors, or assignees are restrained from altering the physical nature of the suit property or creating third-party leasehold or freehold rights till further orders. Defendant No. 1 is specifically directed to deposit the original Registered Deed of Assignment dated 14.08.1998 on or before the next date of hearing: 18.04.2025.

[Para 5] Issue formal process notice to Defendant Nos. 3 and 4 returnable by 18.04.2025 upon Plaintiff furnishing civil process fee within three days.

Sd/-

(S. R. KADAM)

Civil Judge Senior Division, Court Hall 4, Pune
